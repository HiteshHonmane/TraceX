# TraceX Vision

> **Find the signal. Eliminate the noise.**

# Why TraceX?

Developers spend countless hours searching through logs, reproducing issues, and switching between outdated debugging tools.

Serial terminals haven't evolved in decades. They stream text, but they don't help developers understand what they're seeing.

TraceX exists to change that.

---

# Vision

Build the world's best developer platform for debugging, tracing, and understanding connected systems.

Our goal is simple:

> Help developers find anything from their logs with unmatched speed, precision, and clarity.

Every feature we build should reduce debugging time and increase developer productivity.

---

Replace outdated serial terminals with a modern native debugging workspace.

Expand into a complete debugging platform supporting:
